01 / Batch creation

Import a list.
Export the variations.

Import CSV, TSV, or Excel, or paste a list. Match columns to the design’s fields to create one video per row.

Create up to 200 variations per batch, changing text, numbers, and colors while keeping the design consistent. Track progress in Exports and retry failed videos.

02 / Music & sound

Add music.
Set the mood.

Music & sound

Give your video
a soundtrack.

Choose bundled music or upload MP3, M4A, WAV, or OGG. Adjust music and effects separately, loop a track, and fade out at the end.

  • Preview a sound before choosing it.
  • Check the mix in a motion preview.
  • Keep sound in MP4, WebM, and MOV exports.

PNG and GIF exports are silent. Audio uploads can be up to 50 MB and ten minutes long.
