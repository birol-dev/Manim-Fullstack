What your agent
can do.

Connect a compatible AI client through MCP to browse templates, edit saved projects, inspect storyboards, and queue exports.

Use Reload saved in an open project to see the agent’s changes.

Connection setup

Set up
the connection.

  1. 01

    Open Connect to AI.

    Choose Connect to AI in Studio to see your installation’s connection details.

  2. 02

    Add Studio to your client.

    Copy the MCP setup into your AI client. Keep credentials private.

  3. 03

    Try a project.

    Ask for a template and a saved project. Review it in Studio before exporting.

Connection details

What stays local.
What your client sends.

Projects stay on your computer. Your AI client may send information to its provider according to its settings.

Source installs need Studio running. The desktop app also supports a background connection that starts its service for the agent.
