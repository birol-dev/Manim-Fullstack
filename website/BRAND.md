# Manim Studio mark

The primary mark is **refracted light**: three flowing bands that bend together,
like layers of a shader. Coral suggests highlights and heat; violet suggests glass
and gradients; cyan suggests water. It uses no letter or play-button shorthand.
The silhouette remains distinct without its gradients.

- `assets/logo.svg`: full-color shader icon with coral, violet, and cyan bands.
- `assets/logo-light.svg`: white transparent mark for dark or scenic backgrounds.
- `assets/logo-dark.svg`: dark transparent mark for light backgrounds.
- `logo.svg`: horizontal wordmark for wider spaces.
- `favicon.svg`: the primary icon in a compact browser-ready SVG.
- `assets/logo-concept-orbit.svg` and `assets/logo-concept-prism.svg`: two alternative
  directions retained alongside the chosen refracted-light mark.

The website, Studio navigation, browser icon, and Windows icon use the primary mark.
Keep its proportions, leave at least one quarter of the symbol width clear around
it, and use the single-color version where the gradient would lose contrast. The
favicon can be used at 16 px; use the full icon at 32 px or larger when possible.
